Enterprise Data Foundation telemetry, as part of Cisco's telemetry data offerings, enables enhanced visibility of data for product adoption, up sale and cross sell across Cisco's enterprise portfolio. It collects and streams telemetry data from various Cisco products, such as Catalyst Center, Cisco Firewall, WebEx, Data Center product to name a few to provide insights into device performance, usage, and potential risks. This telemetry data helps customers and Cisco proactively identify opportunities for sales and CX to improve overall product experience.

Key Aspects Include

Collection of performance and operational data from customer product deployment and management platforms.
Enabling analytics to understand feature utilization and customer satisfaction.
Supporting proactive risk mitigation campaigns and customer enablement through targeted communications.
Secure collection and handling of telemetry data with customer privacy considerations.

Develop and integrate with Web Services, Micro Services, and APIs to ingest and expose data from various internal and external sources.
